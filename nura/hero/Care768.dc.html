Care balance

Her circle fills it. Nura spends it on her.

Care balance is the friendly name for what her people have funded, and what is still coming. A sister starts it, others add to it, and she never sees a number — she sees what shows up.

Her sister starts it
Eleven others add to it
Dinner at six tonight, from Priya.
She is told who it came from. She is never told what it cost.
What her people have funded
already arrived
still coming
Dinners that arrive
Priya, and three others
A sitter who comes
Her mum
A visit that gets booked
The Thursday group
Nura spends it as it is needed.
No one is shown what anyone gave
How the balance behaves
She is never shown what is left.
She is never asked to add to it.
When it runs low, her circle is told. Only them, and quietly.
The only number she ever sees is the time dinner arrives.
