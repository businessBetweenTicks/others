Motion specification

14.0 seconds, plays once when the stage enters view. Nine beats: a message arrives, the scene beside it, one word back, three things resolve, a worry clears, the weight comes off. Storyboard frames 01–09 sit to the right.

A plum rule marks the one beat that is her — the one-word reply. A moss rule marks the rest state. Even here the accents are 3px underlines, never fills; the rule holds in documentation as well as on the page.
Easing tokens · three curves, no others
ease-rise
cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.30, 1)
Entrances: bubbles, cards, captions. Fast out of the gate, long settle.
ease-settle
cubic-bezier(0.33, 0, 0.12, 1)
Things finding their place: rotation to zero, the final lift, the tick drawing.
ease-soft
cubic-bezier(0.40, 0, 0.20, 1)
Anything that only changes opacity or colour. Never moves anything.
Durations · four values, and nothing in between
220ms
quick — hover, focus ring, and every offset between two things starting
420ms
base — card fills, colour changes, dimming, the tick drawing
620ms
calm — every bubble and card entrance, every rotation to zero
900ms+
long — the plate settling, the worry warming, the final lift at 1200
Every animating duration in frames 01–09 is one of those four. Stagger is always 130ms and offsets are always 220ms. Travel is always 14px — the same distance for a bubble arriving, a card settling and the whole composition lifting. Scale is used exactly once, on the plate in frame 03. Holds are the only free numbers, because a hold is an absence of motion rather than a duration of it.
Never, in any frame
No overshoot, spring or bounce on any property.
No confetti, sparkle, pulse or looping ambient drift.
No typing indicator, chime, badge or unread count.
No progress bar, counter, or anything resembling a number going up.
No baby, pram, or nursery object enters frame at any point.
No auto-loop. It plays once; a quiet Replay appears after the hold.
How it behaves at each width
1440+
All nine beats in the 6-column stage, in the storyboard's three columns: the photography plate filling the left 58%, her thread on the phone over it, the cards resolving on the open field at the right.
1280
Identical sequence and identical composition, stage scaled to 520px tall. No timing changes at all.
768
The stage drops below the copy and gets wider than it is on laptop — 672px against 564. Same three columns, same nine beats; the plate keeps its 58% and the cards spread into the extra room rather than the phone growing.
375
Two things go: the phone silhouette, because she is already holding one, and the photography plate, because at 327px it would be a stamp. The thread plays directly on the field, bubbles arriving top to bottom, then the three cards resolve in place beneath a hairline. The photograph returns further down the page as the section's own image. Sequence shortens to 11s by trimming the holds, never the entrances.
Reduced motion, and the first paint
prefers-reduced-motion: reduce — no sequence. The frame 09 composition renders immediately as a still, complete and legible. Nothing is lost: the rest state already says the whole thing. This is the fallback, not a degraded version.
First paint — that same still is the poster image, and it is what every hero artboard shows. It renders with the page; the sequence starts only once the stage is 50% in view. The hero never waits on the animation to become readable.
Text alternative — the stage carries a description for screen readers: “A message arrives with her name. She replies with one word. Her six-week visit is booked, dinners are arriving three evenings a week, and Thursday afternoon is hers.”
Build handoff
Everything here is DOM and CSS except the photograph — no video, no canvas. Bubbles, cards and the tick are markup; the tick is a stroked SVG animated on stroke-dashoffset. If it is built as Lottie instead, 60fps, and the nine beats stay on the frame boundaries above so either build reads identically. Total motion weight target: under 40KB excluding the photograph.
