Plum

The act of giving, and her voice in a thread. The primary button, her outgoing bubbles, one editorial mark per page. Nothing else.
Moss
#6E7A61 · oklch .545 .035 130
Something is handled. The tick, the eyebrow above a headline. Never a fill, never a badge, never a large area.
The accent budget. Plum and moss together never exceed 5% of the painted area of any screen — roughly what you see in that bar. This is the single rule that makes the palette read as expensive rather than merely muted. When a section feels flat, the fix is space and type, never more colour.
Typeface · two families, and one rule about who speaks
Aa
Newsreader
Variable, optical size 6–72, weights 300–500 plus italic. Warm, low-contrast, slightly bookish — editorial without being a fashion serif. Fallback: Iowan Old Style, Palatino, Georgia.
Used for her: headlines, her words, the First Week Check scene, pull quotes, package names.
Aa
Instrument Sans
Weights 400–600. Neutral, a touch warm, no tech-startup tells. Fallback: Helvetica Neue, Helvetica, Arial.
Used for Nura: body copy, what was booked, chased, filed, covered. Labels, navigation, buttons, prices.
The rule
Serif is her voice. Sans is the work. The two never mix inside one block of text, and the split is never explained on the page — it just holds, section after section, until the reader feels it. It is what lets the First Week Check page carry a mother's account and a logistics ledger on the same spread without either one flattening the other.

Surface
Radius 2px — everything except message bubbles. Square-ish reads editorial; rounded reads app.
Radius 14px, one corner 4px — message bubbles only. The one soft shape on the site, reserved for the thread.
Lift — 0 14px 34px −22px rgba(28,26,23,.32). One shadow, used only where a thing has arrived. Never on buttons, never on hover.
Primary button — Plum on Ground, 20/34 padding on desktop, full-width 56px on phone. Hover darkens to #3B1F33 over 220ms. One per screen.
