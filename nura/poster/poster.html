On making “data forward” reassuring rather than technical

Don’t tell her the data is safe — show her. What she shares, what she gets back, and who never sees it. And put the buyer’s own name on that “never sees it” list, because “not even you” is the most reassuring thing the page can say.

The thread
Your six-week visit is Tuesday at 10. Someone will be there for the baby. ok Dinner lands at six tonight.

She replies with one word. No app, no login, nothing to check.

The line
Stays with her Her delivery record Her insurance
Never sees any of it The giver Her doctor

A dash, never a cross — it was never on the list, not refused.

The balance
Dinners that arrive A sitter who comes

Counted in things, never in money. Four dinners is not 66% of anything.

Motion, built

Three easing curves, four durations, 14px of travel. The trust barrier finishes drawing before the people on the far side appear. Reduced-motion removes all of it and loses nothing.
